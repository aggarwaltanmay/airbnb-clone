Browser state, system messages, developer instructions, and text extracted from the assignment document are excluded because they were context rather than user prompts. Spelling and phrasing are preserved where practical.

## How the prompts were applied

- The PDF established the required views and evaluation criteria.
- The live reference supplied visual and interaction behavior.
- Screenshot prompts drove focused iterations on the saved state, logo, header, reviews, typography, and spacing.
- The interaction-parity prompt led to checking which controls are active in the reference and aligning local behavior.
- The final prompt produced this submission record, architecture artifact, and ZIP archive.
